# Privacy Policy — Neon Cell Survival

**Last updated: 11 August 2026**

Neon Cell Survival ("the app") is developed by Abidos.

## Data we collect

**None.** This version of the app does not collect, store, transmit, or share
any personal data. It contains no analytics, no advertising, and no crash
reporting.

## Network access

This version of the app works entirely offline and does not request the
Android `INTERNET` permission. No data leaves your device, because the app
never makes a network connection.

## Local storage

Your best score is saved locally on your device so it persists between
sessions. This value never leaves your device and is deleted when you
uninstall the app.

## Permissions

The app requests a single permission, `WAKE_LOCK`, which prevents the screen
from dimming while you are playing. It is used for no other purpose.

## Advertising

This version of the app does not display advertising. A future version is
planned to include ads served by a third-party advertising provider. When
that version is released, this policy will be updated beforehand to name the
provider, describe the data it collects (typically advertising identifiers
and approximate device information), and link to its own privacy policy. The
Google Play data safety disclosure for the app will be updated at the same
time.

## Children

This version of the app contains no data collection, advertising, or in-app
purchases. The app is intended for users aged 13 and over.

## Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated whenever the app's data practices change, and
always before the version introducing the change is released. The "last
updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
